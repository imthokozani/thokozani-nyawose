Student Instructional Leader (SI Leader) Attendance Management System

An independent software solution designed to eliminate manual attendance registers and streamline session tracking for university SI Leaders.

The Administrative Challenge

SI Leaders traditionally relied on manual sign-in sheets and repetitive Excel spreadsheet entry to track student attendance across academic support sessions.

The Automated Solution

An SI Leader generates a unique session QR code; students scan the code on their devices, complete a digital attendance form, and records are automatically captured for easy management.

My Specific Contributions (Sole Developer)

  • Identified the real-world administrative pain point and designed the end-to-end digital check-in workflow.
  • Implemented dynamic QR code generation per instructional session.
  • Built the digital student attendance submission form and structured attendance record management.
